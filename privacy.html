ProjectSignal privacy notice

Effective October 22, 2026; updated October 27, 2026 (website and AI tailoring). ProjectSignal is operated by Ryan Smith, doing business as ProjectSignal, 5015 S Regal St #H1060, Spokane, WA 99223.

What we collect about subscribers: business name, contact name, email address, an optional phone number, the job preferences and company website you give us on the sign-up form, how and when you agreed to receive the digest, billing status, and a log of which digests were sent to you. Payment card details are handled by our payment processor, Stripe, and never stored by us.

Why: to send the digest you asked for, to tailor it to your business, to bill for it, to honor unsubscribes, and to keep a record that we only email people who opted in.

Tailoring from public records and your website: when you sign up, we look up public City of Seattle permit records that list your company as the contractor, and read the public pages of the website you give us. From that and your answers we write a short profile of your shop (the work you do, building types and typical job sizes) and use it to rank your list. We don't share it with anyone.

AI: we use Claude, an AI system from Anthropic, to read your answers, your website and the permit records, write your shop profile, and rank each week's projects for you. Each list is checked against the City's official records.

What we don't do: sell or rent your information, or add you to other mailing lists.

Where it lives: on our own computers and with the providers we use (Google Workspace for email and the sign-up form, Stripe for payments, Anthropic for the AI that tailors your list).

How long: while you subscribe, and up to 2 years after for billing and legal records. If you unsubscribe, we keep your email address on a do-not-email list so you are never emailed again.

Your choices: stop the emails any time with the "Stop these emails" link (or, for paying subscribers, by cancelling with the "Manage or cancel your subscription" button) in every email; ask for a copy or deletion of your information at info@projectsignalhq.com.

Permit data: the digest itself contains public-domain City of Seattle permit records, which may include business names and addresses published by the City.
